Bio

About

Kyle Webb is a data scientist and data engineer with over ten years of experience spanning cost analysis, machine learning, and applied statistics across scientific research and industry. He holds a Master of Science in Statistics from Virginia Tech University. His professional interests center on the practical application of artificial intelligence and machine learning, with particular attention to the healthcare and financial markets. Outside of work, he is a husband and father who enjoys North Carolina beaches and spending time outdoors.

Career

After earning his graduate degree in Statistics in December 2016, Kyle began his career at Technomics Inc., where he performed cost estimation and analysis reporting in support of the U.S. Air Force and Navy. In 2018, he joined the National Institutes of Health as a data science fellow, subsequently serving as a bioinformatician with the National Human Genome Research Institute (NHGRI).

Today, Kyle is a Staff Data Scientist at eSimplicity, where he supports the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) in managing enterprise data operations, data lineage, and analytics — enabling thousands of end users to process terabytes of healthcare data. He also serves as an AI Data Analyst Fellow with eSimplicity's Rapid Innovation Center, where he prototypes applied-AI solutions, including an AI-agent cost-analysis application.
